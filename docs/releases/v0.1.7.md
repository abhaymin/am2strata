**am2Strata v0.1.7**: computer use (a sandboxed desktop the chat can drive, with rules you choose), a fuller
browser, narration dubbed into other languages, and a quality check for media.

## Install or update

**Linux (x86_64) and macOS (Apple Silicon):**

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/abhaymin/am2strata/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
```

**Windows (x64), in PowerShell:**

```powershell
irm https://github.com/abhaymin/am2strata/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex
```

Running it again over an earlier version keeps your models and settings.

## New

### Computer use (off by default)
- **Settings › Computer and browser use:** switches for Browser use and Computer use, both off until you turn them on.
- **Rules you choose:** presets **Strict** (the default), **Creator** and **Custom**. Strict never types into password
  or payment fields and asks before anything that sends, deletes, installs, buys or publishes. Every step is checked
  against the rules before it runs; a refused step is never run and says which rule stopped it.
- **Modes:** *sandbox* (a private virtual screen, never your real desktop), *watch-only* (looks, never clicks or types),
  *assist* and *trusted-job*. One approval covers a job; a job stops at 200 steps (`computer.max_steps`).
- **The sandbox's own screen:** started on first use and stopped after 10 idle minutes, refusing network
  connections. Needs `xvfb` and `xdotool` on Linux; without them the tools are simply not offered. macOS and Windows
  desktops come later.
- **"Using the computer / browser"** shows while a chat is at work, with a Stop button.

### The browser
- **More steps:** `browser_tabs` (list, switch, close; the last tab stays), `browser_select` for dropdowns, and
  `browser_wait` for text to appear (up to 30 s).
- **Site allow and block lists** (`computer.site_allow` / `computer.site_block`); a blocked site is refused before
  it loads, and the block list wins.
- **Downloads** are refused unless you set a download folder. With one, files land only there, with safe names, are
  never opened, and Strict asks before keeping each.

### Media
- **`dub_narration`:** narration translated and spoken in another language, timed to the original beats, then mixed
  under the music as a second audio track.
- **`check_media`:** checks a video or song the way a reviewer would: length, frame rate, still or frozen stretches,
  loudness peaks and silence, and the words actually heard against the script.
- **The video maker:** thumbnails render without a font installed, and the media-job hourly limit message is clear.
