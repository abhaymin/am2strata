**am2Strata v0.1.3**: songs, speech in your language with native voices, deeper chats, and a Monitor that pages.

## Install or update

**Linux (x86_64) and macOS (Apple Silicon):**

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/abhaymin/am2strata/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
```

**Windows (x64), in PowerShell:**

```powershell
irm https://github.com/abhaymin/am2strata/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex
```

Running it again over an earlier version keeps your models and settings.

## New

- **Chat can dig deeper:** an answer may take 100 tool rounds (searches, pages, tools), up from 8. The run config's
  `mcp.max_rounds` sets the default, and the chat settings' **Tool rounds per answer** (or `strata_max_rounds`) up to
  500. On long runs, older tool results are shortened before the conversation outgrows the model's context, and the
  answer says so.
- **Speech in the language of the text:** `/v1/audio/speech` finds the language itself (`language: "auto"`, the
  default) and hands it to a model that speaks it. The web app's Text to speech gets a Language picker, and Read aloud
  speaks each answer in its own language. Speech may now be up to 20,000 characters (about 20 minutes).
- **Native female and male voices** (`voice: "female"` or `"male"`; Laura and Lea are female, Jon and Gary male) in
  Spanish, French, Portuguese, Polish, German, Italian, Dutch and Hindi, made by Parler-TTS mini multilingual and Indic
  Parler-TTS, loaded in Rust. Indic Parler-TTS also speaks Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and other Indian
  languages.
- **Each sentence of speech is checked:** Whisper hears it, and a take that adds or drops words is made again.
- **Songs** (ACE-Step 1.5, ported to Rust): a **Song** tab under Media Generation and `POST /v1/audio/music`. Write
  lyrics, pick one of 32 genres (Bollywood, bhajan, ghazal, qawwali, Carnatic fusion, Malayalam melody, bhangra, pop,
  rock, jazz, lo-fi, cinematic and more) or describe the sound, choose the vocal language and a length up to 10
  minutes. On an RTX 5060 Ti a minute of song takes about 12 seconds. Several takes can be made, keeping the one whose
  lyrics come through best.
- **Chat makes songs and music:** with media on, the chat model can call `make_song` (sung) and `compose_music`
  (instrumental), as it calls `generate_image`.
- **Monitor:** the request history comes in pages (25 to 200 a page), and the table scrolls inside its card.
- **Settings › Media models** gets a Music filter, and speech models can be downloaded from the web app again.

## Fixed

- The media engine no longer gives an answer to the wrong request after a client goes away mid-job (it showed as a
  400 or a speech job that never ended).
- Chatterbox speech that ran on with babble at the start or end of a sentence.
- A media route (`/api/media/genres`) answered without the API key; every route needs it.

## Good to know

- Songs, video, images and the new voices need the build from source with an NVIDIA card (the media engine is not
  in the downloads; see the README).
- Songs are an early version: the song planner is still to be ported, so sung words do not always follow the lyrics
  (best on songs of about a minute; try another take). Hindi and Spanish sing well; Malayalam less clearly.
